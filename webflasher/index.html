Claudeputer Web Flasher

Flash the firmware to your Cardputer directly from this page — no toolchain, no Arduino IDE. One image works on both the Cardputer 1.1 and the Cardputer ADV (the board is auto-detected).

Illustration of the on-device chat UI (not a photo).

⚠️ Your browser can't flash over USB. Use desktop Chrome or Edge. ⚠️ This page must be served over HTTPS to flash.

ChromeEdge desktop · Web Serial API required

How to flash

  1. Plug the Cardputer into your computer with a USB-C cable.
  2. Click Connect & Install and pick the serial port (USB JTAG/serial or CP210x/CH9102).
  3. If nothing is detected, hold G0 (the top button) while plugging in to enter download mode.
  4. Wait for the install to finish, then the Cardputer reboots.

First boot — on-device setup

No secrets are baked into this firmware. On first boot the Cardputer shows a setup screen:

  1. Type your WiFi SSID, press ENTER.
  2. Type your WiFi password, press ENTER.
  3. Paste/type your Anthropic API key (get one here), press ENTER.

Settings are saved on the device (NVS). To reconfigure later, type /setup and press ENTER, or hold G0 while powering on. Your key stays on the Cardputer and is sent only to api.anthropic.com.
